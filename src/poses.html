The animation editor

The disc came with a small tool called MANEDIT and a collection of text pose files. Its nineteen-part figure is built inside the executable. We recovered those parts and the original joint motion so you can play the sequences here.

Drag to turn the figure, scroll to zoom, or scrub through a movement with the slider. Single motions stop at the last pose; walking cycles repeat.
