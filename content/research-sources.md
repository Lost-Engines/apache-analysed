# Article sources

Paths below refer to the sibling apache-reverse research repository. These notes are for maintaining the article, not part of the published site.

- `analysis/media-comparison.md`: releases, engine dates, duplicate discs, no CD audio.
- `analysis/executable-map.md`: appended ELF/DWARF, symbol counts, source paths, original instruction harness.
- `analysis/models/format.md`, `catalogue.md`: model programs, LOD chains, attachments, classification.
- `analysis/terrain/format.md`: grid, features, placements, the missing outer-feature drawing calls.
- `analysis/missions/format.md`, `index.json`: mission data and collection totals.
- `analysis/media-art/usage.md`, `index.json`: palette source usage, iF-16 identification, revision dates, RGB export commands.
- `analysis/poses/format.md`, `loader-trace.md`, `hind-connection.md`: MANEDIT construction, pose formats, playback, Hind file hashes and active infantry animation loader.
- `analysis/cockpits/format.md`: artwork assembly and original views.
- `analysis/physics/format.md`: realistic/Arcade models, torque, failures, fuel-weight bug, comparison results.
- `analysis/audio/format.md`: sampled music, briefings, stream resumption.
- `apache-cpp/README.md`, `apache-redux/README.md`: current reconstruction features and Redux fixes.

Original-renderer screenshots are imported from `analysis/reference/native-renderer`. Model, terrain and animation images are captured from this site's viewers. No generated illustration is used as evidence of game output.

Contemporary sources for MANEDIT and Hind:

- [Scawen Roberts interview, Planet Black & White, archived July 2001](https://web.archive.org/web/20010720140340/http://www.planetblackandwhite.com/features/interviews/scawen.shtml): Roberts identifies his work on Hind's animated soldiers.
- [Hind manual](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/2577400/manuals/9489c65a7d0af953bb520cfc57f048726f84a8a7/HINDmanual.pdf?t=1757289970), printed pages 3 and 26: introduction of ground troops and deployment/boarding.
